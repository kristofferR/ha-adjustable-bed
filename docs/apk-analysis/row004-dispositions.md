connecting callback (`0x38bb84` to `cleanDevice@0x38bbec`) also supplies evidence
for required safe role invalidation and owned operation teardown.

The app omits the library's MTU argument. FlutterBluePlus supplies default
SMI 1024, integer 512, at `0x247a24`; after connection it calls
`requestMtu@0x247e48`, constructs `BmMtuChangeRequest` with 512 at `0x248068`,
then Android `U/i.java:2140..2156` forwards the map's `mtu` to
`BluetoothGatt.requestMtu`. This is Android platform negotiation policy, not a
bed payload, acknowledgement or peripheral-required minimum. HA/Bleak and
proxies own negotiation. This profile neither requests nor claims MTU 512.
