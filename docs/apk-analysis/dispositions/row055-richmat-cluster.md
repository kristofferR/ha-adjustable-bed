## What changed

- Five explicit bed types (`richmat_revive`, `richmat_best_mattress`, `richmat_blvd_home`, `richmat_harmony`, `richmat_idealbed`) with a generated per-app model catalog (`richmat_mh_catalog.py`, checked by `tools/generate_richmat_mh_catalog.py --check`). The protocol variant is `auto` (the app's first-four-character name rule) or the app's picker, wizard or manual model.
- The app's GATT selection, session (version query and VER0/VER1 initialization lists), notification classifier and page gates. The replies are persisted as a capability snapshot and reload the entities when they change.
- Per-model covers (KEEP at the app interval, STOP 120 ms after release), presets, memories and app-labelled buttons; LED and button-light colour and timeout; snore intervention; detection; smart set lock; VER1 angles, massage intensity, motor mode and memory arrival; the waist mattress page.
- Actions `richmat_mh_alarm` (countdown, combined position/massage opcodes, three-slot models with clock sync), `richmat_mh_aroma` and `richmat_mh_waist_alarm`.
- Setup and options forms report names the app cannot resolve (QRRM, Idealbed Cool Touch, unknown or missing names) on the protocol variant.

## Upgrade safety

- The profiles are opt-in bed types. Detection never selects them; the legacy Richmat and RMControl controllers, variants and entities are unchanged.
- Each side of a two-address pair keeps its own app model; the shared options form refuses a model change (`richmat_mh_unpair_first`).
