## What changed

- New explicit bed types: **Jordan's Tranquil app** (`tranquil`), **Customatic Z-Series app (Z-230)** (`customatic_z230`) and **(Z-280)** (`customatic_z280`). The Z-Series app makes the user pick the model, and advertisements cannot tell them apart.
- The Serenity controller became a table-driven `OkinBeddingAppController`. Serenity keeps its exact table, `serenity_` keys and timing.
- Tranquil: Serenity-like surface with a touch Lounge preset and Save Lounge chord, no TV. Literal Remote Selector 4/5 buttons, voice-only waves, massage off and discrete light on/off.
- Z-Series: Z-230 adds combined head and foot motion and a combined massage-intensity button; Z-280 adds Selector 5 (neck artwork, `0x10`/`0x20`), M2 and head/foot massage cycles. Both expose TV, Save ZG/TV and anti-snore. There are no voice frames.
- Z-Series alarm: when the manufacturer string is exactly `CST13`/`CST14`, `zseries_set_alarm` and `zseries_sync_clock` send the app's clock, alarm and status-query frames. Enabling requires both a time and a wake mode.
- New hold services `tranquil_hold_control` and `zseries_hold_control`. Z-Series button presses are bounded by the configured pulse count (default one second, 1-600 pulses accepted and clamped), since the app streams until release. Save buttons hold five seconds per each app's help.
- The voice "stop" Head Up anomaly (Tranquil C43) is excluded for safety; STOP is retained.

## Release-note impact for existing users

None for existing entries. The three bed types are new and manual-only: discovery, auto-detection, the shared OKIN controllers and every existing Serenity entry keep their behavior, entity IDs and unique IDs. Owners of these beds who set them up as another OKIN type can switch to the matching profile in the integration options; the other profile's app buttons and diagnostic sensors are removed automatically.

For real users after a beta or release: which actuator each selector and the Z-230 combined control drives, the ATT write mode and CCCD acceptance, repeat and STOP arrival, save persistence and minimum hold time, massage and light semantics (including the help text's colour cycling), Z-Series alarm and clock behavior (HA sends the clock and alarm frames back-to-back, where the app separates them by a user tap), and notification contents. None of these blocks the static implementation.
