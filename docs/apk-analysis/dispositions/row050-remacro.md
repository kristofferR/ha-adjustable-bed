## What changed

- The controller now exposes exactly the control screen the app selects from the lowest advertised company ID, instead of one generic four-motor surface. The selector is cached as `remacro_model`.
- A protocol variant selects the app: `auto`/`slumberland`, `the_brick` or `jeromes`. It drives the serial counter rules, Jerome's model list and LED setting visibility, wave wrapping and The Brick's OneActivity combined timing.
- Movement sends one press and the axis STOP 120 ms after the hold, with the OneActivity triple-STOP and streaming schedules. The previous controller repeated every press ten times and always sent `0x0001`.
- Presets were wrong: Flat is `0x0111`; `0x0301`/`0x0302`/`0x0303` are anti-snore, TV and Zero-G. Preset re-tap sends STOP. Memory is screen-specific (0, 1 or 2 slots).
- Split beds (company IDs 51 and 52) get a Control side select and the side-specific codes, including the literal `0x2401`.
- Massage uses the apps' head, foot and wave buttons with their counters. Light is an on/off switch; Slumberland and The Brick add the LED level number and save button. RGB color, heating, LED modes, tilt and memory 3/4 were removed.
- App-local state (counter, side, active preset, massage counters, LED level) lives in a per-bed session that survives reconnects, as the static app state does, so a reconnect cannot silently switch sides.
- Notifications are subscribed on connect and only recorded for diagnostics.
- Card: the side select renders in the position section; the save button joins the light buttons.
