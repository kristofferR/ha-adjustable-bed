| D042-OS-01 | EXCLUDED | E02, E04, E17: Android BLE/location permissions, GPS/provider checks, adapter initialization, and legacy scan calls are companion-phone OS setup. Home Assistant's Bluetooth adapter, proxy, and setup infrastructure remains supported. |
| D042-UI-01 | EXCLUDED | E08, E13, E15: unsafe app touch-handler side effects are not reproduced. mode and characteristic properties; **DV2**, physical axes and preset/save,
light, and massage meaning; **DV3**, the physical meaning of notification
byte 10; **DV4**, receiver repeat, STOP, and reconnect behavior. These are

unsafe save chord, or dead branch above is part of the implementation.
