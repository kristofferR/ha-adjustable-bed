## Product and safety boundaries

The following exact scopes require `EXCLUDED` dispositions in the final ledger:

- Bed Wi-Fi, SSID/password provisioning, cloud/accounts and bed network/group
  administration, including `AT+GROUP` / `AT+REBIND` over BLE. This does not
  exclude mixed direct-control framing/session/state or HA Bluetooth adapters.
- TEST endurance automation and RMCS motor sequencing/transmission configuration,
  because they automate prolonged actuation or rewrite diagnostic motor mappings.
  These paths are reachable where selected, not falsely labelled dead.
- Proven package-disabled LP, video, alternate alarm, voice recognition, dormant
  SDK/MTU/bond/auth/OTA/fan and unrelated phone/camera/UI operations, at their
  exact accepted source cuts. Shared supported BLE effects stay in scope.
- Unsafe continuation after lost ownership/cancellation, cached characteristic
  reuse across physical sessions and interleaved diagnostic queues. HA uses its
  coordinator lock, current session and bounded command cancellation. No extra
  hardware opcode, queue-full policy, retry, authentication or ACK is invented.

The source's mutable global `curMode` is read by each `Utils.sendData`
invocation, while mode-click callbacks can replace it during a held worker.
HA deliberately excludes cross-destination continuation and release loss:
each admitted gesture captures its mode and selector context through cleanup.
New local choices apply to the next gesture. Native selector choices survive
HA's connection-owned controller handoff in the address/app/layout state slot;
pending payloads, worker mode and physical sessions do not. Unexpected disconnect
invalidates the session and publishes the app status as disconnected. These
concurrency adaptations add no hardware commands or physical-side claims.

Aborted saves also clear abandoned worker count/latch state so a later gesture
cannot fire early. Cancelled settings do not persist unsent values or consume
an error confirmation. Source cross-profile/global selector inheritance is
excluded: explicit native choices are scoped to the address, app and layout.
Desk error confirmation requires a newly reported callback and does not claim
hardware recovery. The last reported error remains visible afterward. Direct
desk holds cease their 200 ms refresh on cancellation/link loss and never resume
on reconnection; the 13 unsafe source lifecycle traces retain their exact
expected states and scoped exclusions, with implemented wire controls linked.
