# SUTA

**Status:** Partly tested (the bed frame controls from 4.x are tested; the newer app profiles need testing)

**Credit:** Reverse engineering by [kristofferR](https://github.com/kristofferR/ha-adjustable-bed).

## Known Models

SUTA bed frames, RBHC, MOON mattresses and TEMP heating controllers. The exact
Bluetooth name selects the panel. Owners have tested Dreams Sleepmotion /
SUTA-B202B (WLT8016_S106) bed frames; the other models and the newer controls
are not yet tested on hardware.

## Choosing a profile

Choose the app you use with the bed. The rest of this section describes
SUTA Smart Home. For trueMotion, see its [setup instructions](#truemotion).

Enter the complete, case-sensitive Bluetooth name in setup or Configure.
The display title does not select a panel. Names shorter than nine characters
select no controller. Unknown long SUTA names select the app's M666 fallback.
Drawer-only movement is outside bed support.

## Wire codec

### Transports

For trueMotion's FFF0 transport, see [Setup](#setup).

For SUTA Smart Home, the **full name** selects FFE0 for MOON, TEMP, RBHC, DRAWER, STORAGE, SOFA,
YOGABED and ROLLSOFA prefixes; other names select FFF0. The UI uses only the
first nine characters plus explicit B202D, B206A/H and M510D decisions.
For example, SUTA-YOGA and SUTA-YOGABED select the same panel but different
services.

The final characteristic with write or write-without-response properties is
the writer. Every other notify/indicate characteristic is subscribed.
The write branch takes precedence on a characteristic with both properties.
No fixed characteristic UUID is assumed. Normal BED writes use response mode.
Notification setup and MTU negotiation are not an authentication handshake; the app requests MTU 250, without gating the command UI on success.

### Framing and checksum

P1 encodes the complete text plus CRLF as UTF-8. There is no escaping,
checksum, side substitution or segmentation.

P2 encodes a 53 59 header, group, operation, zero high length byte, payload
length, payload, additive sum of the entire preceding packet modulo 256,
and a 54 43 suffix. MOON and TEMP share framing but have separate parsers.

### Responses

Diagnostics and support bundles carry the connection-scoped observations.
Binary callbacks use complete notification frames; malformed short input is
ignored safely, without assuming a different packet or reassembling fragments.

RBHC reads signed big-endian 16-bit values at offsets 1, 3, 5 and 7.
Only the first BACK and the third FOOT field are known to be degrees.
N520's similarly shaped parser is not fed by its normal name route.

MOON reports massage, strength (shown by the side's massage intensity number,
which shows a sent level until the next report), timer (shown by the side's massage timer
select the same way; 0 while massage is off shows unknown), adaptive mode/stiffness, automatic
checkbox state, pressure floats, temperature, reset state, leak number and
version. Pressure values retain their reported side and have no inferred
unit. Temperature floats use the source's one-decimal HALF_UP rounding.
A full-state checkbox update is observed without automatically echoing a write.

TEMP reports power, light, side setpoints/timers, current temperatures,
water and circulation. Version is accepted only on A0/81; its outgoing
version query remains A0/01. MOON's version decoder separately accepts A0/01.
Signed byte observations retain their signed values. When the
controller confirms that heating was turned on, both setpoint numbers show
30 °C, as the app's display does, until the next reported setpoint.

P1 date and alarm events require CRLF in the current callback, as in the app.
Version uses an accumulated buffer. Fragmented date/alarm events are not
promoted to complete messages. Alarm attributes preserve the source record,
weekday, key, time, loop and enabled fields.

### STOP and release behavior

Motion repeats immediately and at the selected panel's own interval, with
a finite elapsed deadline. Slow writes do not create a stale refresh backlog.
Release uses the exact panel STOP burst with a fresh cancellation signal.
Cleanup and notifications retain their captured physical BLE session.

Source fragment lifecycle cancellation adds no STOP and does not erase
already-queued writes. HA separately owns the explicit gesture's release and
drains submitted writes before freeing the scheduler lane. GlobalScope source
jobs are not treated as fragment-owned jobs.

The saved Bluetooth name belongs to one physical bed. Generic motor count,

#### Controls

Native entities expose each panel's controls under the app's button text.
TWO is "Back and foot" where the panel also has back and foot keys, and plain
"Up"/"Down" where it is the only motion; B702's BOTT key is "Storage lift".
Lock/unlock, MOON automatic adjustment and TEMP heating power and light are
switches. Lock state is not reported, so its switch shows the last command.
Version, date and alarm queries, the post-connect state queries and the
unlabelled RBHC/N520 angle commands stay available through the SUTA action
only. Diagnostics carry the decoded observations. B702 offers four programmable
memory slots, B803 three; other panels retain their own exact slots.
Preset M8 does not imply eight programmable memories.

Adaptive soft buttons explicitly select all five modes. Stiff/moderate
changes require a received current adaptive mode for the selected side.
No default mode is invented from a shared UUID or parser.

Dedicated Save actions complete the panel's finite packet burst after its countdown,
with cancellation, session fencing and per-write timeouts. User-specified holds
keep their elapsed deadline, which can end a burst before its final packet.
A short finite hold
recalls the corresponding preset; reaching the threshold starts programming.
B905 starts and resets at four seconds: 3.2 seconds recalls, while programming
writes occur at four seconds and 200 ms later, subject to cancellation and
the BLE schedule. N520's programming burst retains its separate schedule.

MOON starts state, temperature and version queries after the common 300 ms
delay. HA makes one observation request per field. TEMP sends its state
query after 300 ms and version after another 300 ms.

#### Notifications

Only fields the bed's name and reply format provide are published.
State is cleared on disconnect. Old callbacks and delayed actions cannot
write to a replacement connection.

#### Services

`adjustable_bed.suta` accepts an action key from the generated catalog.
Text DT/LV/fan actions use the app's `suffix`; MOON/TEMP actions use
`logical_side` (`left` or `right`) and `value` when their payload requires them.
The physical paired `side` remains separate from the binary payload side.
Starting the head or foot massage zone puts the overall level back to 0,
as the app's shared strength display does.

Text layouts take alarms through the shared `set_alarm` action. Each chosen
weekday becomes one `AT+SA` frame (Sunday 0 on the wire) with the app's Loop
flag `01`, so the alarm repeats weekly. Without weekdays the alarm is set once,
on the next day the time occurs, with Loop `00`. Turning an alarm off sends
`AT+CA` with the same days and time. The app's delete frame is not offered.
Each write is followed by the alarm list query. The clock (`set_date`) is set
with the shared [`sync_clock`](../SERVICES.md#sync_clock) action, using Home
Assistant's current local time; the SUTA action does not accept it.

`snore_cycle` sends the app's normal SNORE burst, then FLAT after the
source's delay. This explicit HA operation owns both effects and cancellation;
it performs no phone sensing or background reconnection.

schedules, so those six controls are withheld.

#### Controls

Use the direction controls the layout provides, including combined movement
and the T/L control on B603 and B903. The card supports held
movement. The memory and preset buttons only appear where the layout offers
them. The card uses Set mode for numbered memories and asks for confirmation
before overwriting a named preset.

Massage has separate Head and Foot timer buttons and a shared level button.
The timers cycle through codes `00`, `20`, `33`, `50`. The level cycles
through `00`, `10`, `20`, `30`. The values are app codes with unverified
physical units. They follow the steps sent by Home Assistant, with no
receiver readback. B202 sends Head level even when its timer is off and sends
Foot timer off when Foot is inactive. On B206A, B903 and M510, active Head
updates immediately and active Foot follows a 150 ms delay. On B603, active
zones get repeated writes and Foot starts 50 ms after Head when both are
active.

Supported layouts also provide an under-bed light, a child lock or an
**Enable beep** button. Light and lock state are assumed. On a standalone receiver, enable the diagnostic
**Read firmware version** button to ask the receiver for its firmware text.

#### Timing

A tap, or a finite press too short for its first refresh, sends one motion
frame. A hold refreshes at the interval in the table
and sends at most 400 refreshes. The Android app can schedule delayed STOP
after an early tap or gesture release. It ends the refresh stream at its cap.
Home Assistant sends the three-frame motor release at the end of a tap or
hold while the same link is live. On editors with a dedicated Stop
control, Stop all sends one motor STOP and then turns off the two massage timers,
with Head requests at 150/250 ms and Foot at 200/300 ms. A motion release only
stops the motors.

Save requests wait three seconds and start the burst after another 100 ms.
Standalone saves send three frames. Split saves send two per addressed
receiver. Saves are separate from held movement.

#### Notifications

The receiver can return firmware text over notifications. GA and GD
observations have no known control consumers or physical meanings for
trueMotion. Motor position feedback is unavailable.

#### Services

Use [Hold control](../SERVICES.md#hold_control) with a direction listed for
the layout, for example `back_up` or `combined_down`. Stop all uses the
dedicated STOP sequence on layouts that offer one. Set alarm, Set bed clock
and Rename Bluetooth name are unavailable on this profile. Rename the device
in Home Assistant to change its display name.
